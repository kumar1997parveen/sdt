About Us

S.D. TEAM HR is a professionally managed manpower solution company with over 1 year of experience in delivering reliable, efficient and scalable workforce solutions. We specialize in providing skilled and unskilled manpower to factories, industrial units, offices and business establishments.

Our objective is to support organizations by supplying disciplined, productive and dependable workforce solutions that enhance operational efficiency and business performance.

We offer comprehensive manpower and working solutions, including:
